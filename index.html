About

I'm Principal Scientist, leading Computational Biology and Data Science at mBiomics, where I built the function from the ground up and now lead a team of eight.

We deliver the R&D data platform, multi-omics and AI/ML methods, and the regulatory-facing analytics behind pre-IND work for live bacterial therapeutics in immuno-oncology.

Earlier, I was scientific lead for computational biology at Eagle Genomics and held research roles at IGB, MPI-AGE, DIfE and MDC. At DIfE I worked on cardiometabolic disease in the EPIC-Potsdam cohort, using Mendelian randomization and omics biomarkers to study causal drivers of disease risk.

My research interests include antimicrobial resistance genomics (ResistomeDB), cfDNA methylation as a liquid biopsy biomarker, and genomic language models.

I hold a PhD in Computational and Systems Biology from FIOCRUZ. I'm Brazilian, born in Rio de Janeiro, and based in Barcelona after ten years in Germany.
